Privacy policy

Last updated 10 November 2026.

The short version

Kesvo does not collect, send or sell any personal data. There is no account, no analytics and no advertising. Everything you record stays on your device.

What is stored, and where

  • Your settings (weight, FTP, 5 km time, units) and training plan progress: in the app's storage on your device.
  • Session summaries for history, ghost racing and the weekly rings: in the app's storage on your device.
  • Activity files (.tcx) and share card images (.png): saved to your device when a session ends. They go somewhere else only if you share them yourself, for example to Strava.

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored. Past sessions can also be deleted in the app.

Bluetooth

Kesvo uses Bluetooth only to connect to sensors you choose: a smart trainer, treadmill, foot pod, heart rate strap, Zwift Click, or the Swimcord app on your Garmin watch. Readings from them are used live and in your saved sessions on the device. Android lists this as location permission on older versions; Kesvo never reads your location.

Network

Kesvo itself collects no personal data, has no account, analytics or ads, and your workouts stay on your phone. Network and app-to-app connections happen only for the features below:

  • Cast to TV (Google Cast): when you tap Cast to TV, Kesvo uses Google Play services (Google Cast) to connect to Google's servers and your Chromecast so it can show the ride on your TV.
  • Health Connect: if you turn on "Send finished sessions to Health Connect" in Settings, Kesvo writes each finished workout (its type, start and end time, and distance) to Health Connect on your phone, where other apps you have connected can see it. It is off until you turn it on, Kesvo never reads your health data, and you can remove Kesvo's access in Health Connect at any time.
  • Garmin watch (Garmin Connect IQ): to count swim strokes from your Garmin watch, Kesvo talks to the Garmin Connect app on your phone.
  • Challenge links and sharing: Kesvo opens challenge links and the system share sheet only when you choose to use them.
  • Update check (direct APK only): the APK downloaded from this website checks this site's latest.json for a newer version. That request carries no personal data (GitHub, which hosts this site, may keep standard server logs; see GitHub's privacy statement). The Google Play version does not check latest.json; Google Play updates it.

Children

Kesvo is not aimed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.
